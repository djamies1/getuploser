Get Up, Loser

Privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026.

Get Up, Loser is a free app that sends you rude notifications about your step count. It has no accounts and no servers that store your data. This policy explains what the app reads, what it keeps, and the one optional thing it can send.

What stays on your phone

  • Your settings: the name you choose, your tormentor, meanness level, schedule and step goal. Stored on the device only.
  • Your step count. On Android the app reads steps from Health Connect; on iPhone it reads Motion & Fitness data. It uses them only on the device, to choose and fill in roasts, sitting alerts and the morning recap.
  • Notifications are scheduled on the device. Nothing is sent from a server.

The app never sends your step data, health data, name or roasts anywhere. It doesn't use them for advertising, doesn't sell them, and doesn't share them with anyone.

Health data

The app reads only step counts. It doesn't write to Health Connect or Apple Health. You can withdraw access at any time in Health Connect (Android) or under Settings, Privacy & Security, Motion & Fitness (iPhone). The app keeps working without it, using time-of-day roasts.

Optional anonymous usage stats

Off by default. If you turn on Share anonymous usage stats in the app, it sends a small set of events to Aptabase, an open-source analytics service: when the app opens, when setup finishes, when a roast is shared or refreshed, when a notification is opened, and which setting changed. Events can include your tormentor, meanness level and whether profanity is on. Aptabase also records the app version, operating system, language and a random session ID that changes regularly.

Usage stats never include step counts or any health data, your name, your roasts, your location or any device identifier, and they can't be linked back to you. Turn them off at any time in Settings, under Privacy.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app makes an image of the current roast and opens your phone's share sheet. Nothing is shared until you pick where it goes. Shared images always address the roast to "loser", never the name you set.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Anonymous usage stats can't be tied to you, so there is nothing personal to delete.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13. Its humor, and its optional profanity, are for older teens and adults.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
